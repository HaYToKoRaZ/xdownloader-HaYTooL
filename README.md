## 🇬🇧 English

### Overview
**XDownloader HaYTooL** is a powerful, lightweight, and privacy-focused browser extension for downloading videos, photos, and animated GIFs from X (Twitter) in original, highest quality with a single click.

No login required. No third-party servers. All operations happen directly and locally inside your browser.

### ✨ Key Features
- **⚡ One-Click Instant Download:** Sleek download button embedded right under tweets.
- **💎 Original Quality:** Maximum resolution available (1080p, 4K for videos; full original images).
- **🏷️ Customizable Filename Patterns:** Auto-name files using Tweet ID, username, timestamp, date, or hash.
- **📁 Subdirectory Management:** Automatically categorize downloads into subfolders by username or custom path.
- **📜 Built-in History Search:** Review and search your past downloads with the internal history manager.
- **🌗 Dark & Light Themes:** Modern UI with customizable themes to match your preference.
- **🌐 Multi-Language Support:** Instant language switching with flag options (Turkish, English, Japanese, Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Korean).
- **🔒 Privacy First:** Your data and downloads never leave your browser. Zero tracking.

### 📥 Manual Installation
1. Download `XDownloader-HaYTooL-v...-WebStore.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/HaYToKoRaZ/xdownloader-HaYTooL/releases/latest).
2. Extract the archive into a permanent folder.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` in your browser.
4. Enable **Developer mode** in the upper-right corner.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted directory.
